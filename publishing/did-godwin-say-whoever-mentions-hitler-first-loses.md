# Did Godwin say whoever mentions Hitler first loses?

Just show me the answer

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** Mike Godwin's 1990 law says only that a long online argument is likely to produce a comparison to Hitler or the Nazis. The rule that whoever makes the comparison loses was added later by the Usenet community, not by Godwin.

Tell someone: Godwin's 1990 law says only that a long online argument is likely to produce a Hitler comparison; the loser rule came later, from Usenet.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version treats the "loser" rule as part of Godwin's law: whoever invokes Hitler ends the argument and loses it.
