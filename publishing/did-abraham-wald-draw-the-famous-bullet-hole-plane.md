# Did Abraham Wald draw the famous bullet-hole plane?

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** Abraham Wald did not draw the bomber covered in red dots. The record describes that image as a designer's later recreation, a teaching device from decades after the war.

Tell someone: Abraham Wald's 1943 memoranda were dense probability, not a sketch, and the red-dotted bomber is a designer's later recreation, drawn decades after the war.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version comes with a picture. A plane seen from above is spattered with red dots where returning bombers were hit. Wald, studying it, tells the military to armor the clean areas instead.
