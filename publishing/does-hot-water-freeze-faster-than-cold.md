# Does hot water freeze faster than cold?

Just show me the answer

DISPUTED

**Disputed.** Whether hot water can freeze faster than cold is unresolved. Erasto Mpemba and Denis Osborne published the claim in 1969, a careful 2016 study by Burridge and Linden found no evidence for a meaningful effect, and the record says the dispute remains open.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version is simple: hot water freezes first, and the effect carries Mpemba's name.
