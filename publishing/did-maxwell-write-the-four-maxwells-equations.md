# Did Maxwell write the four Maxwell's equations?

Just show me the answer

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** James Clerk Maxwell built the theory, but the four compact equations that carry his name are Oliver Heaviside's reformulation of 1884. Maxwell's own 1865 paper contained twenty equations.

Tell someone: Maxwell built the theory, but his 1865 paper had twenty equations; Oliver Heaviside condensed them into the famous four in 1884.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version holds that Maxwell wrote down the four equations taught today, in the vector form printed in textbooks.
