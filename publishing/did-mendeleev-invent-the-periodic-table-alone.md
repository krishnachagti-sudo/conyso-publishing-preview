# Did Mendeleev invent the periodic table alone?

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** Dmitri Mendeleev published his periodic table in 1869, but he did not conceive it alone. John Newlands had proposed a related law of octaves in 1865, and Julius Lothar Meyer worked out a similar arrangement independently at about the same time.

Tell someone: Mendeleev had company: Newlands proposed a related law of octaves in 1865, and Lothar Meyer reached a similar table independently at about the same time.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version gives the table to one man in one year: Mendeleev, 1869.
