# Is "Muphry's law" misspelled on purpose?

Just show me the answer

TRUE

**True.** The misspelling is deliberate. John Bangsund, of the Society of Editors (Victoria) in Australia, coined Muphry's law in the society's March 1992 newsletter as "the editorial application of the better-known Murphy's law."

Tell someone: Muphry's law is misspelled on purpose: John Bangsund coined it in 1992 so the law's name would carry its own error.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version takes the name for a slip: someone misspelled Murphy's law and the error stuck.
