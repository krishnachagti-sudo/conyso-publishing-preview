# Did the Sydney Opera House open a decade late and vastly over budget?

Just show me the answer

TRUE

**True.** The Sydney Opera House was estimated to open in 1963 at a cost of a few million pounds. A scaled-down version opened in 1973 at A$102 million. The exact figures vary by source.

Tell someone: The Sydney Opera House opened a decade late, in 1973, scaled down and costing more than a hundred million, though exact figures vary by source.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version holds the Opera House up as the classic runaway project. Famous stories of waste tend to grow in the retelling, so it is fair to suspect this one has too.
