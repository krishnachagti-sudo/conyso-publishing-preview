# Does Laffer himself say the Laffer curve wasn't his idea?

Just show me the answer

TRUE

**True.** Arthur Laffer has disclaimed originality for the curve that bears his name. He credits earlier sources, including Ibn Khaldun in the fourteenth century and John Maynard Keynes.
