Story No. 81 · Round 9: Discovery stories

Was Photo 51 taken by Raymond Gosling?

Your call: true, folklore or disputed?

How sure?

TRUE

True. Raymond Gosling, a doctoral student, took Photo 51 at King's College London in May 1952, while working under Rosalind Franklin's supervision. The credit belongs to both of them.

Tell someone: Photo 51 was taken by Raymond Gosling, a doctoral student, in May 1952, using Rosalind Franklin's fibers and working under her supervision.

What's the version people tell?

The popular version gives the photograph to Franklin alone. She is the person the image is most closely associated with, and the short telling of the DNA story has no room for her student.

What's the source?

Cited in the book: "The story behind Photograph 51," King's College London. "Photo 51," Wikipedia. Cobb and Comfort, Nature (2023). The full list is on the Law Tome's entry.
