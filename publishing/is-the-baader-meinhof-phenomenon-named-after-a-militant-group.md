# Is the Baader–Meinhof phenomenon named after a militant group?

Just show me the answer

TRUE

**True.** The nickname comes from the West German militant group. It traces to a 1994 reader submission to the *St. Paul Pioneer Press* from a writer who had heard the group's name twice in quick succession.

Tell someone: The Baader–Meinhof phenomenon is named after the West German militant group, by a 1994 newspaper reader who heard its name twice in quick succession.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular assumption is understandable. Two surnames joined by a dash have the look of a scientific eponym, and it is easy to suppose that two researchers stand behind the effect.
