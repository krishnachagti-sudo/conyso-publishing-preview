# Did Rousseau coin "the noble savage"?

Just show me the answer

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** Jean-Jacques Rousseau did not coin "the noble savage." The phrase does not appear in his writings and was pinned on him later.

Tell someone: The phrase "noble savage" appears nowhere in Rousseau's writings; it was pinned on him later, and he hedged his view as a thought experiment.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version makes the phrase Rousseau's own summary of his view: people in a state of nature were noble, and civilization ruined them.
