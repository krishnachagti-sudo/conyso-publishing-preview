# Was Schrödinger's cat meant to make a quantum idea look absurd?

Just show me the answer

TRUE

**True.** Erwin Schrödinger devised the cat in 1935 as an objection to the Copenhagen interpretation of quantum mechanics. His aim was to show the absurdity of extending quantum superposition to a large object, not to claim that a cat can be alive and dead at once.

Tell someone: Schrödinger's cat was a critique: he devised it in 1935 to show that extending quantum superposition to a large object is absurd.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version turns the argument around: Schrödinger proposed that a cat in a sealed box really is both alive and dead until someone looks.
