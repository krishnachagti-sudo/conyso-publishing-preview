### [Does the Coriolis effect decide which way a sink drains?](https://conyso.com/publishing/does-the-coriolis-effect-decide-which-way-a-sink-drains.html)

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** At the scale of a sink, bath or toilet, the Coriolis effect is thousands of times too weak to set the direction of draining. Water drains both ways within the same hemisphere.

Round 6 · No. 53

### [Is the uncertainty principle just about measuring disturbing things?](https://conyso.com/publishing/is-the-uncertainty-principle-just-about-measuring-disturbing-things.html)

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** The uncertainty principle is not merely the idea that measuring a particle disturbs it. It says a quantum state cannot have sharply defined values of both position and momentum at once, and it holds even for perfect instruments.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/is-the-uncertainty-principle-just-about-measuring-disturbing-things.html)

Round 6 · No. 54

### [Was Schrödinger's cat meant to make a quantum idea look absurd?](https://conyso.com/publishing/was-schrodingers-cat-meant-to-make-a-quantum-idea-look-absurd.html)

TRUE

**True.** Erwin Schrödinger devised the cat in 1935 as an objection to the Copenhagen interpretation of quantum mechanics. His aim was to show the absurdity of extending quantum superposition to a large object, not to claim that a cat can be alive and dead at once.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/was-schrodingers-cat-meant-to-make-a-quantum-idea-look-absurd.html)

Round 6 · No. 55

### [Did vortex shedding bring down the Tacoma Narrows Bridge?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-vortex-shedding-bring-down-the-tacoma-narrows-bridge.html)

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** The 1940 collapse of the Tacoma Narrows Bridge is more accurately explained by aeroelastic flutter, a distinct, self-excited mechanism. The record says the case should not be presented as simple vortex-street resonance.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-vortex-shedding-bring-down-the-tacoma-narrows-bridge.html)

### [Does air over a wing speed up to meet the air underneath?](https://conyso.com/publishing/does-air-over-a-wing-speed-up-to-meet-the-air-underneath.html)

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** Air does move faster over a wing's upper surface, and the pressure there is lower. But the popular reason, that the air must hurry to rejoin the air beneath, is a misconception that NASA debunks.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/does-air-over-a-wing-speed-up-to-meet-the-air-underneath.html)

Round 6 · No. 57

### [Does hot water freeze faster than cold?](https://conyso.com/publishing/does-hot-water-freeze-faster-than-cold.html)

DISPUTED

**Disputed.** Whether hot water can freeze faster than cold is unresolved. Erasto Mpemba and Denis Osborne published the claim in 1969, a careful 2016 study by Burridge and Linden found no evidence for a meaningful effect, and the record says the dispute remains open.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/does-hot-water-freeze-faster-than-cold.html)

Round 6 · No. 58

### [Does a broken bone heal back stronger?](https://conyso.com/publishing/does-a-broken-bone-heal-back-stronger.html)

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** There is no evidence, contrary to popular belief, that a healed fracture becomes stronger than the original unbroken bone. The claim is an overstatement of Wolff's law, which describes how healthy bone adapts to the loads placed on it.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/does-a-broken-bone-heal-back-stronger.html)

Round 6 · No. 59

### [Did Thomas Young shine light through two slits?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-thomas-young-shine-light-through-two-slits.html)

DISPUTED

**Disputed.** Thomas Young showed that light interferes, in his Bakerian Lectures around 1801 and 1803. Whether he performed a literal two-slit experiment is disputed: his demonstrations used sunlight, pinholes and narrow cards.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-thomas-young-shine-light-through-two-slits.html)

Round 6 · No. 60

### [Was the Doppler effect tested with trumpeters on a train?](https://conyso.com/publishing/was-the-doppler-effect-tested-with-trumpeters-on-a-train.html)

TRUE

**True.** C. H. D. Buys Ballot tested the Doppler effect for sound in 1845, using trumpeters on a moving train. Christian Doppler had predicted the effect three years earlier, in an 1842 paper in Prague.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/was-the-doppler-effect-tested-with-trumpeters-on-a-train.html)
