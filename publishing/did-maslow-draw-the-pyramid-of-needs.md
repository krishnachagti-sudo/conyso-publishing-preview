# Did Maslow draw the pyramid of needs?

Just show me the answer

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** Maslow did not draw the pyramid. The famous diagram was created by later authors, not by Maslow himself.

Tell someone: The hierarchy of needs is Maslow's, but the pyramid came from later authors, and his own ordering was less rigid than the shape.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version is a five-tier pyramid credited to Abraham Maslow, with physiological needs at the base and self-actualization at the tip, each layer to be completed before the next.
