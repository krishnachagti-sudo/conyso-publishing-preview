The series

Two series, two datasets

Every Conyso Publishing book belongs to the dataset it is built from.

Series one

The Law Tome series

Built from the Law Tome, a free directory of 1,116 named laws, effects and principles. Each law there has what it says, who it is named after, where it came from and how well it holds up, with sources. The books in this series take that record somewhere a screen can't go: a dinner table, a quiz night, a classroom.

Series two

The Bias Atlas series

Built from the Bias Atlas, a free map of 544 cognitive biases: what each one is, how to spot it, and whether the research behind it held up. No titles are announced yet. They will be listed here when they are ready.

How does a dataset become a series?

A series keeps the dataset's habits. Each entry names its source, links back to its record online, and goes through the same checks. The record is the source of truth: when a book and its record disagree, the record is checked again and one of them is corrected.
