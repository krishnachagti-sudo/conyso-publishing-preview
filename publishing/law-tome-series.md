# The Law Tome series

The Law Tome series turns [the Law Tome](https://conyso.com/lawtome/), a free directory of more than a thousand named laws, effects and principles, into books. Every law in the directory names its sources, and every book keeps that habit: each answer names the source that settles it and links back to its law.
