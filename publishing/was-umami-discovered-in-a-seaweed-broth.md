# Was umami discovered in a seaweed broth?

Just show me the answer

TRUE

**True.** In 1908 the Japanese chemist Kikunae Ikeda isolated glutamate from kombu seaweed broth, coined the word umami and developed MSG as a seasoning. Umami is now widely accepted as the fifth basic taste.

Tell someone: Umami was found in kombu seaweed broth: Kikunae Ikeda isolated glutamate there in 1908 and named the taste before developing MSG.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version is skeptical. On this telling, umami is a marketing invention: a word made up to sell a seasoning, not a taste in its own right.
