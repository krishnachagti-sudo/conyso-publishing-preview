Story No. 19 · Round 2: Who really said it?

Did Twain or Einstein say "A man with one watch knows what time it is"?

Your call: true, folklore or disputed?

How sure?

FOLKLORE

Folklore. Neither did. The saying first appears without a name in the San Diego Union in 1930. The attributions to Mark Twain and Albert Einstein are unsupported, and no documented Segal wrote it either.

Tell someone: Neither Twain nor Einstein said it: the one-watch line first ran, with no author's name, in the San Diego Union in 1930.

What's the version people tell?

The popular version credits the line about the man with one watch and the man with two to Twain or to Einstein.
