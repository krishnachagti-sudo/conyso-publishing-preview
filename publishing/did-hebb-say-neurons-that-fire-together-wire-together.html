Story No. 12 · Round 2: Who really said it?

Did Hebb say "neurons that fire together wire together"?

Your call: true, folklore or disputed?

How sure?

FOLKLORE

Folklore. Donald Hebb set out the rule in 1949. The slogan is not his. It came in 1992, usually credited to Carla Shatz as a mnemonic, and crediting it to Hebb is, in the record's words, the most common error about the rule.

Tell someone: Hebb set out his rule in 1949, but the rhyme "fire together, wire together" came in 1992 and is usually credited to Carla Shatz.

What's the version people tell?

The popular version puts the rhyme in Hebb's mouth, as his own summary of how learning works in the brain.
