# Did Philippa Foot invent the trolley problem?

Just show me the answer

TRUE

**True.** Philippa Foot introduced the original case in 1967, in a paper called "The Problem of Abortion and the Doctrine of the Double Effect." She did not name it. Judith Jarvis Thomson called it the trolley problem and added its best-known variants in 1976 and 1985.

Tell someone: Philippa Foot set out the trolley case in 1967, but the name belongs to Judith Jarvis Thomson, who also added the footbridge variant.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version comes in two forms. One treats the puzzle as an old one, too old to trace. The other treats it as a modern psychology test.
