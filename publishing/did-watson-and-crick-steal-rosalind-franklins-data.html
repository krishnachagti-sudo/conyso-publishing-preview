Story No. 82 · Round 9: Discovery stories

Did Watson and Crick steal Rosalind Franklin's data?

Your call: true, folklore or disputed?

How sure?

DISPUTED

Disputed. The record documents that Maurice Wilkins showed Photo 51 to James Watson without Franklin's knowledge, and that her data shaped the 1953 model. Recent scholarship argues that this was not a simple story of theft.

Tell someone: Wilkins showed Photo 51 to Watson without Franklin's knowledge; recent scholarship argues the full history was more complex, with Franklin an equal contributor.

What's the version people tell?

The popular version is a heist. Watson and Crick took Franklin's photograph behind her back and won the Nobel Prize on it.
