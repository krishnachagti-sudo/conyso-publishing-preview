Story No. 33 · Round 4: Charts and numbers

Is the famous inverted-U stress curve in Yerkes and Dodson's paper?

Your call: true, folklore or disputed?

How sure?

FOLKLORE

Folklore. The smooth inverted-U curve of stress against performance is not a plot of Yerkes and Dodson's results. Their 1908 paper was a study of mice learning under electric shocks of different strengths.

Tell someone: Yerkes and Dodson's 1908 paper measured no stress and no humans, only how fast forty mice learned under shocks of different strengths.

What's the version people tell?

The popular version is a single bell-shaped curve. Performance rises with arousal or stress to a sweet spot, then falls, and the curve is credited to a 1908 study.
