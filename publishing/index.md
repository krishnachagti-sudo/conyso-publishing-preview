## What does Conyso Publishing publish?

Books that take a famous claim and check it: the named laws people quote, the stories told about how they were found, and the thinking errors that make the stories stick.

Conyso keeps two free reference sets online. [The Law Tome](https://conyso.com/lawtome/) covers 1,116 named laws, effects and principles: the laws of science and everyday life, like Murphy's law or Newton's, not legal statutes. [The Bias Atlas](https://conyso.com/biases/) covers 544 cognitive biases and which findings held up. Every entry in both names its sources. The imprint turns those records into books for a game night, a classroom or a shelf. The datasets stay free; the books are what you read away from a screen.

## How is a book made?

1. **1**From a recordEvery entry starts from a Law Tome or Bias Atlas record, which names its sources.
2. **2**Drafted, then checkedAI drafts the text from the record. Every verdict, quote and date is checked against the sources, and the check is logged.
3. **3**Signed offLiving people get a legal read, the art is reviewed by two people, and the editor approves the final book.
4. **4**Corrected in publicEvery book prints the address of its corrections page. Mistakes found later go there.

### Did an apple fall on Newton's head?

**Folklore.** There is an apple in Newton's own account of his thinking about gravity. It does not hit him on the head. That detail is a later embellishment.

## Questions about the imprint

What is Conyso Publishing?

Conyso's imprint for quiz and reference books. Every book is built from one of Conyso's free, sourced datasets, [the Law Tome](https://conyso.com/lawtome/) or [the Bias Atlas](https://conyso.com/biases/), and checked against the sources before print.

Who is behind it?

Conyso, the company that publishes the two datasets. The books are edited by [Krishna Chagti](https://conyso.com/publishing/about.html#editor), Conyso's founder.

Are the books written by AI?

The text is drafted with AI from the sourced records, and the illustrations are made with AI. Every verdict is then checked against its sources and the check is logged, and the editor approves the finished book. [How we use AI](https://conyso.com/publishing/how-we-use-ai.html) has the detail.

Which books are out?

None yet. The first, [*True or Folklore?*](https://conyso.com/publishing/true-or-folklore.html), is coming soon in paperback and Kindle. Its first round is free to play here.

How do I report a mistake?

Corrections are listed on the [public corrections page](https://conyso.com/lawtome/errata/), which every book prints in its front matter.
