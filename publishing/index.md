### [Did Newton himself tell the apple story?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-newton-himself-tell-the-apple-story.html)

**True.** The apple story is not a fable invented by Newton's admirers. According to *Encyclopaedia Britannica*, it traces back to Newton's own later recollection. Only the apple hitting his head was added afterward.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-newton-himself-tell-the-apple-story.html)

### [Did Galileo drop balls from the Leaning Tower of Pisa?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-galileo-drop-balls-from-the-leaning-tower-of-pisa.html)

**Folklore.** The tower drop is likely a legend. It is not in Galileo's own writings. His documented work on falling bodies used balls rolling down ramps, with notes from around 1603 to 1604 and results published in 1638.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-galileo-drop-balls-from-the-leaning-tower-of-pisa.html)

### [Did Pythagoras discover the Pythagorean theorem?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-pythagoras-discover-the-pythagorean-theorem.html)

**Folklore.** The relation that carries Pythagoras's name was known and used long before him. A Babylonian tablet from around 1800 BC lists its number triples, and Pythagoras lived from about 570 to 495 BC.

### [Did Einstein win his Nobel Prize for something other than relativity?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-einstein-win-his-nobel-prize-for-something-other-than-relativity.html)

**True.** Einstein's Nobel Prize, the physics prize designated for 1921, was for his work on the photoelectric effect. NobelPrize.org gives the citation as "for his services to Theoretical Physics, and especially for his discovery of the law of the photoelectric effect." Relativity is not what the prize named.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-einstein-win-his-nobel-prize-for-something-other-than-relativity.html)

### [Did Churchill promise "blood, sweat and tears"?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-churchill-promise-blood-sweat-and-tears.html)

**Folklore.** In May 1940 Churchill offered "blood, toil, tears and sweat." That is four items, not three, and "toil" is the word that dropped out.

### [Did Edward Murphy say "Anything that can go wrong will go wrong"?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-edward-murphy-say-anything-that-can-go-wrong-will-go-wrong.html)

**Disputed.** A maxim like it came out of an Air Force rocket-sled project around 1949, and Murphy's name stuck to it. Who first said it, and in what words, is not settled. The earliest firm printing under Murphy's name, from 1952, credits an unnamed physicist.

## How are the books made?

1. **From a sourced record.** Each story starts from a Law Tome or Bias Atlas entry, which names its sources.
2. **Drafted with AI.** The text is drafted with AI from that record.
3. **Checked against the sources.** Every verdict, quote and date is checked against a primary source, or the best one available, and the check is logged on each story's page. A story whose verdict is still open waits.
4. **Illustrated with AI.** The cartoons are made with AI.
5. **Corrected in public.** Mistakes found after print go on the [corrections page](https://conyso.com/lawtome/errata/).

Edited by [Krishna Chagti](https://conyso.com/publishing/editor.html), the founder of Conyso. [His founder site](https://conyso.com/founder/) has the rest of his work.

## Questions people ask

What is Conyso Publishing?

Conyso's imprint for quiz and reference books. Each book is built from one of Conyso's free, sourced datasets: [the Law Tome](https://conyso.com/lawtome/) or [the Bias Atlas](https://conyso.com/biases/).

Is True or Folklore? out yet?

Not yet. It is coming soon in paperback and Kindle. This site will link to the store once it is on sale, and [the Law Tome's books page](https://conyso.com/lawtome/books/) shows its status.

Who writes the books? Is it AI?

The text is drafted with AI from the sourced records, then every verdict, quote and date is checked against a primary source, or the best one available. The illustrations are made with AI. [Krishna Chagti](https://conyso.com/publishing/editor.html) edits the books and signs off each one.

What does DISPUTED mean?

That the record is genuinely unsettled. Where good sources disagree, the book says so rather than picking a side.

What happens if a verdict is wrong?

It goes on the public [corrections page](https://conyso.com/lawtome/errata/), with what changed.

Can I play the stories at a quiz night or in class?

Yes. The rounds on this site are free to play: read the question aloud, let everyone call it, then tap for the verdict. The book adds a two-team scoresheet and a page of classroom ideas.
