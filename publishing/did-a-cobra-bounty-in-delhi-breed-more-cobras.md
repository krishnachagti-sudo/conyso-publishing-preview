# Did a cobra bounty in Delhi breed more cobras?

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** No record shows that anyone in Delhi bred cobras for a bounty. The idea the story named rests on firmer evidence, which is the next story in this round.

Tell someone: The cobra-breeding story, usually set in Delhi, traces to an 1873 report on a Madras bounty saying only that it "was alleged," yet an economist named an effect after it.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version is short. British officials in colonial Delhi paid a reward for dead cobras, and local people started breeding cobras to collect it. The German economist Horst Siebert liked the story enough to name an idea after it. His 2001 book *Der Kobra-Effekt* describes how a reward offered to solve a problem can end up paying people to manufacture it.
