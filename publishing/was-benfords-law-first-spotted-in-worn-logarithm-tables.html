Was Benford's law first spotted in worn logarithm tables?

Your call: true, folklore or disputed?

How sure?

TRUE

True. The astronomer Simon Newcomb first noticed the leading-digit law in 1881, from the observation that the early pages of logarithm tables, those for numbers starting with 1, were the most worn. Frank Benford rediscovered it in 1938.

Tell someone: Simon Newcomb first spotted Benford's law in 1881, noticing that the early pages of logarithm tables, for numbers starting with 1, were the most worn.

What's the version people tell?

The popular version credits Frank Benford with the discovery, as the name suggests.
