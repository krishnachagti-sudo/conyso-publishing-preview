### [Did 38 witnesses watch Kitty Genovese die and do nothing?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-38-witnesses-watch-kitty-genovese-die-and-do-nothing.html)

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** The record finds no basis for the story that 38 witnesses watched Kitty Genovese die and did nothing. At least one witness shouted at her attacker.

Round 3 · No. 22

### [Did the Stanford prison guards turn cruel on their own?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-the-stanford-prison-guards-turn-cruel-on-their-own.html)

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** The guards did not become cruel on their own. Archival recordings and later scholarship show that the experimenters, including the warden, instructed and pressured them to be tough.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-the-stanford-prison-guards-turn-cruel-on-their-own.html)

Round 3 · No. 23

### [Did Hawthorne factory output rise whatever the lighting?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-hawthorne-factory-output-rise-whatever-the-lighting.html)

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** The original Hawthorne lighting data do not show output rising whatever the lighting. When Steven Levitt and John List reanalyzed them, they called the famous patterns "entirely fictional".

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-hawthorne-factory-output-rise-whatever-the-lighting.html)

Round 3 · No. 24

### [Did 65% of Milgram's volunteers give the maximum shock?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-65-of-milgrams-volunteers-give-the-maximum-shock.html)

DISPUTED

**Disputed.** About 65 percent of participants went to the maximum 450 volts in one baseline condition. Across the more than twenty conditions Milgram ran, obedience varied widely, and archival research has questioned how many participants believed the shocks were real.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-65-of-milgrams-volunteers-give-the-maximum-shock.html)

Round 3 · No. 25

### [Did Milgram prove we are all six handshakes apart?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-milgram-prove-we-are-all-six-handshakes-apart.html)

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** Milgram did not prove that everyone is six handshakes apart. Most of the chains in his 1967 experiment never reached their target, and the "six" is a median over the minority that did.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-milgram-prove-we-are-all-six-handshakes-apart.html)

Round 3 · No. 26

### [On most trials, did Asch's subjects go along with the wrong answer?](https://conyso.com/publishing/on-most-trials-did-aschs-subjects-go-along-with-the-wrong-answer.html)

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** On most trials, Asch's subjects did not go along. They gave the group's wrong answer on about a third of critical trials and stayed independent on about two-thirds.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/on-most-trials-did-aschs-subjects-go-along-with-the-wrong-answer.html)

Round 3 · No. 27

### [Does waiting for a second marshmallow predict success in life?](https://conyso.com/publishing/does-waiting-for-a-second-marshmallow-predict-success-in-life.html)

DISPUTED

**Disputed.** Early follow-ups linked a preschooler's ability to wait with later achievement. A larger 2018 replication found the link much weaker and largely explained by family background.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/does-waiting-for-a-second-marshmallow-predict-success-in-life.html)

Round 3 · No. 28

### [Did the Robbers Cave study have a failed first attempt?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-the-robbers-cave-study-have-a-failed-first-attempt.html)

TRUE

**True.** Before the famous 1954 Robbers Cave study, Muzafer Sherif ran a pilot in 1953 that failed. The boys resisted the hostility the researchers tried to induce, and suspected the staff.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-the-robbers-cave-study-have-a-failed-first-attempt.html)

Round 3 · No. 29

### [Was the forgetting curve based on just one person?](https://conyso.com/publishing/was-the-forgetting-curve-based-on-just-one-person.html)

TRUE

**True.** The forgetting curve rests on one subject: Hermann Ebbinghaus, who tested himself. Its best-known modern replication, in 2015, also used essentially one subject.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/was-the-forgetting-curve-based-on-just-one-person.html)

Round 3 · No. 30

### [Do we know what became of Little Albert?](https://conyso.com/publishing/do-we-know-what-became-of-little-albert.html)

DISPUTED

**Disputed.** No one has settled who Little Albert was or what became of him. Rival candidates have been proposed, and the record says there is no settled answer.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/do-we-know-what-became-of-little-albert.html)
