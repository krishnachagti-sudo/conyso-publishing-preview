# Did habeas corpus come from Magna Carta?

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** Habeas corpus did not come from Magna Carta. Its roots predate the charter of 1215. It grew out of English common law over centuries, and the Habeas Corpus Act of 1679 codified it.

Tell someone: Habeas corpus has roots older than Magna Carta; it grew through English common law and was codified by the Habeas Corpus Act of 1679.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version gives the writ a single birth certificate. Magna Carta, in 1215, created the right to make a jailer justify an imprisonment before a court.
