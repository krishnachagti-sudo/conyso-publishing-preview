The editor

Krishna Chagti

Founder of Conyso and editor of Conyso Publishing's books.

What does the editor do?

Krishna Chagti founded Conyso, which publishes the Law Tome and the Bias Atlas as free, sourced datasets. He edits the books built from them.

Editing here means deciding what goes in and standing behind what the book says. The text is drafted with AI from the sourced records. Every verdict, quote and date is then checked against a primary source, or the best one available, and the check is logged. A story whose verdict is still open waits until it is settled. The illustrations are made with AI. Mistakes found after print go on the corrections page.
