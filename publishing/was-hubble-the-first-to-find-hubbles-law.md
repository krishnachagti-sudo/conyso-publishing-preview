# Was Hubble the first to find Hubble's law?

Just show me the answer

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** Georges Lemaître derived the relation between a galaxy's distance and its speed of recession, and estimated its constant, in 1927. Edwin Hubble's paper came two years later, in 1929. In 2018 the International Astronomical Union recommended renaming the law the Hubble–Lemaître law.

Tell someone: Georges Lemaître derived Hubble's law in 1927, two years before Hubble's paper, and since 2018 the IAU recommends calling it the Hubble–Lemaître law.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version credits Hubble alone with discovering, in 1929, that the universe is expanding.
