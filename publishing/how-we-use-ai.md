# How we use AI

Plainly: AI drafts the text and makes the pictures. Here is what happens around that.

## What does AI do?

- **Drafts the text** of each entry from the dataset's record: the claim, its history and its sources.
- **Makes the illustrations**, such as the cartoons in *True or Folklore?*

## What happens around the AI?

- **The record comes first.** A draft may only say what a sourced record supports.
- **Every verdict is checked** against a primary source, or the best available, and the check is logged with what was opened and when. In the free round you can see each story's check.
- **Flagged verdicts wait.** If the check finds a problem it cannot settle, the story waits for the editor.
- **Living people get a legal read.**
- **Every image is reviewed** against a twelve-point list, and a second reviewer sees the full set.
- **The editor approves** the final book and cover.

## What do we disclose, and where?

The store's AI-generated box is ticked for both text and images on every title. Every page of this site carries a one-line disclosure in its footer, and each book's front matter says how it was made. The books are credited to a real editor, not an invented author.

## What if the AI got something wrong?

Then it is our mistake, and it goes on the [corrections page](https://conyso.com/lawtome/errata/) printed in the book. [Our standards](https://conyso.com/publishing/standards.html) say how corrections work.

## Questions about AI

Is the book written by AI?

The text is drafted with AI from sourced records, and the illustrations are made with AI. The checking, the legal read and the editor's approval sit on top of that draft, and the store listing says so.

Does the AI decide the verdicts?

The draft states the verdict the record supports. Every verdict is then checked against its sources and the check is logged; a verdict the check flags waits for the editor, and the editor approves the finished book.

Why use AI at all?

Because it makes it possible to turn a large sourced dataset into many short entries, and to spend the human time on choosing, checking and correcting instead.
