# Did Sturgeon say "Ninety percent of everything is crap"?

Just show me the answer

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** Theodore Sturgeon's printed word was "crud," not "crap." His *Venture Science Fiction* column first printed it in September 1957. In March 1958 the column gave it as "Ninety percent of everything is crud." He also called it his Revelation, not his law.

Tell someone: In print in 1957 and 1958, Sturgeon wrote that ninety percent of everything is "crud," and he called it his Revelation.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version quotes Sturgeon's law in a blunter word: "Ninety percent of everything is crap."
