Round 4 · No. 32

### [Do the least skilled people really overrate themselves the most?](https://conyso.com/publishing/do-the-least-skilled-people-really-overrate-themselves-the-most.html)

DISPUTED

**Disputed.** In 1999 Kruger and Dunning found that the lowest scorers on several tests overestimated their rank. Since 2016 several researchers have argued that much of that pattern is a statistical artifact, and a 2026 reanalysis reports that it inverts.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/do-the-least-skilled-people-really-overrate-themselves-the-most.html)

Round 4 · No. 33

### [Is the famous inverted-U stress curve in Yerkes and Dodson's paper?](https://conyso.com/publishing/is-the-famous-inverted-u-stress-curve-in-yerkes-and-dodsons-paper.html)

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** The smooth inverted-U curve of stress against performance is not a plot of Yerkes and Dodson's results. Their 1908 paper was a study of mice learning under electric shocks of different strengths.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/is-the-famous-inverted-u-stress-curve-in-yerkes-and-dodsons-paper.html)

### [Did Maslow draw the pyramid of needs?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-maslow-draw-the-pyramid-of-needs.html)

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** Maslow did not draw the pyramid. The famous diagram was created by later authors, not by Maslow himself.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-maslow-draw-the-pyramid-of-needs.html)

Round 4 · No. 35

### [Did Abraham Wald draw the famous bullet-hole plane?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-abraham-wald-draw-the-famous-bullet-hole-plane.html)

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** Abraham Wald did not draw the bomber covered in red dots. The record describes that image as a designer's later recreation, a teaching device from decades after the war.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-abraham-wald-draw-the-famous-bullet-hole-plane.html)

Round 4 · No. 36

### [Did Wald really tell the military to armor where returning planes weren't hit?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-wald-really-tell-the-military-to-armor-where-returning-planes-werent-hit.html)

TRUE

**True.** The reasoning in the famous story is Wald's. In his 1943 memoranda he estimated aircraft vulnerability from the damage on returning planes, while accounting for the planes that did not return.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-wald-really-tell-the-military-to-armor-where-returning-planes-werent-hit.html)

Round 4 · No. 38

### [Was Benford's law first spotted in worn logarithm tables?](https://conyso.com/publishing/was-benfords-law-first-spotted-in-worn-logarithm-tables.html)

TRUE

**True.** The astronomer Simon Newcomb first noticed the leading-digit law in 1881, from the observation that the early pages of logarithm tables, those for numbers starting with 1, were the most worn. Frank Benford rediscovered it in 1938.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/was-benfords-law-first-spotted-in-worn-logarithm-tables.html)

Round 4 · No. 40

### [Did Marilyn vos Savant's correct Monty Hall answer draw a storm of objections?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-marilyn-vos-savants-correct-monty-hall-answer-draw-a-storm-of-objections.html)

TRUE

**True.** When Marilyn vos Savant gave the correct answer to the Monty Hall problem in 1990, some ten thousand readers wrote in, close to a thousand of them with doctorates, insisting she was wrong.
