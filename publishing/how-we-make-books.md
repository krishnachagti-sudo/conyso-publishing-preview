# How we make books

From a sourced record to a printed page, with a check at every step that can go wrong.

## What are the steps?

1. Choose the entriesA book starts from a brief and an outline: which records go in, in what order, for which reader. For a quiz book that means a story with a version almost everyone knows and a record that can be checked.
2. Draft from the recordThe text is drafted with AI from the dataset's record, which carries the claim, its history and its sources. The draft may only say what the record supports.
3. Check every verdictEvery verdict, quote and date is checked against a primary source, or the best one available. Each check is logged with what was opened, the date, and whether the draft held, was fixed or was flagged. A flagged verdict waits for the editor.
4. Read for living peopleAny entry that names a living person gets a legal wording pass before print.
5. Make and review the artIllustrations are made with AI, then every image is checked against a twelve-point list and a second reviewer looks at the full set.
6. Rewrite anything too closeThe manuscript is compared with the Wikipedia articles it cites, and any non-quoted run of twelve or more shared words is rewritten.
7. Typeset and proofThe book is typeset, run through a manuscript checker, and proofed in print.
8. Editor approvalThe editor reads the final file and the cover and approves them. Nothing goes on sale without that, because the book goes out in his name.
9. Publish and discloseThe book goes on sale with the store's AI-generated box ticked for both text and images, and its corrections page already live.

## What has to be true before a book ships?

Seven checks. If any one fails, the book slips a week rather than going out with a gap.

1. The manuscript checker passes.
2. Every factual claim is traced to a source in the record, and every verdict and quote is checked and logged.
3. The legal wording pass on living people is done.
4. Every image passes the twelve-point list, and a second reviewer has seen the full set.
5. The cover, description, keywords and categories are written, with the AI disclosure ready.
6. Every web address printed in the book works.
7. The editor has approved the final file and cover.

## Questions about the process

How long does a book take?

Each book moves through five stages over about three weeks: brief and outline, draft and art, checks, approval, then publication. Several books are worked on at once.

What stops a book from shipping?

Any one of seven checks failing. The book slips a week rather than going out with a gap.

Where are the sources?

A short source line sits on each entry in the book, and the full list is on the matching Law Tome or Bias Atlas entry online.
