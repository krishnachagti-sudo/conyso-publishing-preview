# Did Hardin later admit the tragedy of the commons was missing a word?

Just show me the answer

TRUE

**True.** Garrett Hardin later conceded that his error had been to omit one word: "unmanaged." His tragedy describes an unmanaged, open-access resource, not every shared one.

Tell someone: Garrett Hardin later conceded his famous essay left out one word, "unmanaged," and Elinor Ostrom documented communities that manage shared resources successfully.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version treats the tragedy as an iron law: any resource held in common will be ruined unless it is privatized or controlled by the state.
