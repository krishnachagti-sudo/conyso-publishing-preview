# Is the "Mount Stupid" curve from the Dunning–Kruger paper?

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** The "Mount Stupid" curve is not in Kruger and Dunning's 1999 paper. The record describes it as a later internet embellishment.

Tell someone: The "Mount Stupid" curve appears nowhere in Kruger and Dunning's 1999 paper, which compared people's estimated rank with their actual test rank.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version is a picture. Confidence is plotted against experience. It shoots up to a peak labeled Mount Stupid after a little learning, falls as the size of the subject comes into view, and climbs slowly again with real expertise. The chart is usually captioned as the Dunning–Kruger effect.
