# Did Thomas Gresham formulate Gresham's law?

Just show me the answer

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** Thomas Gresham did not formulate Gresham's law. Henry Dunning Macleod named it after him in 1858, and the principle had been stated long before: by Oresme, by Copernicus around 1519, and in Aristophanes' play *The Frogs* around 405 BC.

Tell someone: Gresham did not formulate Gresham's law; Macleod named it after him in 1858, and Aristophanes stated the principle around 405 BC.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version credits Gresham, a Tudor financier, with the observation that bad money drives out good.
