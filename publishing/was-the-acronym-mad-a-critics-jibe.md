# Was the acronym "MAD" a critic's jibe?

Just show me the answer

TRUE

**True.** The acronym MAD is attributed to a critic, Donald Brennan of the Hudson Institute, who used it derisively to argue that the nuclear posture it named was irrational. Robert McNamara, under whom the doctrine took shape, promoted "assured destruction," not MAD.

Tell someone: McNamara called it "assured destruction"; the acronym MAD is attributed to Donald Brennan, a critic who used it derisively, so the initials spell the charge.

## What's the version people tell?

The popular version holds that MAD was the Pentagon's own acronym, a name the planners gave their strategy.
