## Did Newton get hit by an apple? Did Archimedes really shout "Eureka!" in the bath? Did Galileo drop balls from the Leaning Tower?

Everyone has heard these stories. Fewer people know which ones actually happened.

**True or Folklore?** puts 100 famous stories from science, history, money and the mind to the test. Then turn the page for the verdict, the real story, why the myth spread, and the earliest source that settles it.

### Did Archimedes shout "Eureka!" in the bath?

**Folklore.** Archimedes stated the law of buoyancy around 250 BC, in his treatise *On Floating Bodies*. The bath, the crown and the cry of "Eureka!" are almost certainly a later embellishment.

### Did an apple fall on Newton's head?

**Folklore.** There is an apple in Newton's own account of his thinking about gravity. It does not hit him on the head. That detail is a later embellishment.

![Cartoon for the question: Did Newton himself tell the apple story?](https://conyso.com/publishing/art/003.png)

TRUE

Round 1 · No. 3

### Did Newton himself tell the apple story?

**True.** The apple story is not a fable invented by Newton's admirers. According to *Encyclopaedia Britannica*, it traces back to Newton's own later recollection. Only the apple hitting his head was added afterward.

## Questions about the book

Who is True or Folklore? for?

Anyone who likes being the person who knows the real version: quiz hosts, family tables, classrooms and curious readers. It is written as a gift, with a page at the front to write who it is for.

How long does each story take?

About two minutes. The question sits alone on one page and the answer is printed on the back.

How many of the stories are true?

34 of the 100. 48 are folklore, and 18 are still genuinely disputed, which the book says rather than pretending to settle.

What age is it for?

Adults and teens. A few stories deal with a murder, war and psychology experiments that harmed people, so read a round before using it with younger students.

Can I try it before it is out?

Yes. [Round 1](https://conyso.com/publishing/true-or-folklore-round-1.html) is free to play here, with each story's verdict and the sources it was checked against.

What formats will it come in?

Paperback and Kindle. In the Kindle edition each answer is behind a link from its question. There is no store link yet because it is not on sale yet.
