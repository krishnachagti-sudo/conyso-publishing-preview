# The Bias Atlas series

The Bias Atlas series will turn [the Bias Atlas](https://conyso.com/biases/), Conyso's free map of cognitive biases, into books: thinking errors, how to spot them and which findings held up. Like the Law Tome series, every book is built from sourced records and checked against them.

## Which books are in it?

No titles announced yet. They will be listed here when they are ready.

The other series is [the Law Tome series](https://conyso.com/publishing/law-tome-series.html). Its first book, [*True or Folklore?*](https://conyso.com/publishing/true-or-folklore.html), is coming soon.
