# Was the Overton window named only after Overton's death?

Just show me the answer

TRUE

**True.** Joseph P. Overton developed the idea in the mid-1990s, but it was his colleague Joseph Lehman who named and elaborated it, after Overton died in a 2003 ultralight-aircraft crash.

Tell someone: Joseph Overton developed the idea, but his colleague Joseph Lehman named it the Overton window only after Overton died in 2003.
