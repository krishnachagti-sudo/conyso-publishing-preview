Round 10 · No. 92

### [Did a French bounty on rat tails in Hanoi backfire?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-a-french-bounty-on-rat-tails-in-hanoi-backfire.html)

TRUE

**True.** In 1902 the French colonial administration in Hanoi paid a bounty for each rat killed, with a severed tail as proof. Rat-catchers began cutting the tails off live rats and letting them go to breed. The case is documented in a 2003 paper by M. G. Vann in *French Colonial History*.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-a-french-bounty-on-rat-tails-in-hanoi-backfire.html)

### [Did more efficient steam engines make Britain burn more coal?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-more-efficient-steam-engines-make-britain-burn-more-coal.html)

TRUE

**True.** In *The Coal Question* (1865), the economist William Stanley Jevons observed that England's coal use soared after more efficient steam engines expanded coal's industrial uses.

Round 10 · No. 95

### [Did broken-windows policing cause New York's 1990s crime drop?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-broken-windows-policing-cause-new-yorks-1990s-crime-drop.html)

DISPUTED

**Disputed.** The claim that broken-windows policing caused New York's crime decline is disputed and confounded. Crime fell across most American cities in the 1990s, including cities that never adopted the approach, and a 2006 analysis by Harcourt and Ludwig found little causal support for the crime-reduction claim.

[The answer and its source →](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-broken-windows-policing-cause-new-yorks-1990s-crime-drop.html)

### [Did the Sydney Opera House open a decade late and vastly over budget?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-the-sydney-opera-house-open-a-decade-late-and-vastly-over-budget.html)

TRUE

**True.** The Sydney Opera House was estimated to open in 1963 at a cost of a few million pounds. A scaled-down version opened in 1973 at A$102 million. The exact figures vary by source.

Round 10 · No. 99

### [Did Hardin later admit the tragedy of the commons was missing a word?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-hardin-later-admit-the-tragedy-of-the-commons-was-missing-a-word.html)

TRUE

**True.** Garrett Hardin later conceded that his error had been to omit one word: "unmanaged." His tragedy describes an unmanaged, open-access resource, not every shared one.

Round 10 · No. 100

### [Did Garrett Hardin invent the overgrazed-pasture parable?](https://conyso.com/publishing/did-garrett-hardin-invent-the-overgrazed-pasture-parable.html)

FOLKLORE

**Folklore.** The overgrazed-pasture parable came from William Forster Lloyd in 1833. Garrett Hardin named the idea and popularized it in 1968, 135 years later.
