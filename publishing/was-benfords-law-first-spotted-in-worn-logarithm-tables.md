# Was Benford's law first spotted in worn logarithm tables?

Just show me the answer

TRUE

**True.** The astronomer Simon Newcomb first noticed the leading-digit law in 1881, from the observation that the early pages of logarithm tables, those for numbers starting with 1, were the most worn. Frank Benford rediscovered it in 1938.
