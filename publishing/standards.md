# Our standards

The rules every Conyso Publishing book follows, written down so you can hold us to them.

## What do the verdicts mean?

TRUE

The record supports the story as usually told.

FOLKLORE

The famous version is wrong, misattributed or added later, and the record shows what really happened.

DISPUTED

Good sources disagree, or the evidence is not there to settle it. We say so rather than pick a side.

## How are sources handled?

Every entry names its source on the page, with the earliest source that settles the question where one exists. The full list sits on the matching Law Tome or Bias Atlas entry online. Every verdict is checked against a primary source, or the best one available, and the check is logged. Where the check cannot settle a verdict, the story waits.

Wording is our own: any non-quoted run of twelve or more words shared with a cited Wikipedia article is rewritten.

## How are living people treated?

Any entry that names a living person gets a legal wording pass before print. Criticism is attributed to the named source that makes it, and claims about a person's motives are left out.

## What will we never do?

- NEVERInvent a source, a quote, a date or a number.
- NEVERFake scarcity, countdowns or "was" prices. A print-on-demand book never runs out.
- NEVERBuy, swap or gate reviews, or route only happy readers to a store.
- NEVERCall a book a bestseller, or put other authors' names in our titles.
- NEVERPre-tick add-ons, shame a reader for saying no, or hide a subscription.
- NEVERCite effects that failed to replicate as if they had not.
- NEVERClaim social proof we do not have. Until there is real data, a myth is "widely believed", never "what most people think".

## What about reviews?

One neutral request at the end of a book, the same for every reader. Nothing is offered in exchange, and no reader is sorted by how happy they seem before being asked.

## How do corrections work?

Every book prints the address of its [corrections page](https://conyso.com/lawtome/errata/) in the front matter. When a mistake is found, the correction goes there with what changed, and the next printing and the ebook are fixed. A correction is not hidden or quietly edited away.
